    Where the rest could come from

    Oroberyl: daily activity, the weekly routine, version 1.7 events, redeem and livestream codes, Talos-II chests (15–60 each), Etchspace Salvage (1,800 in total) and SKPORT's web sign-in.

    Permits: Bond Quota buys up to 5 current-banner tickets at 15 each, or Chartered permits at 25 each. Duplicates give 50 Bond Quota per 6★ and 10 per 5★. The AIC Quota exchange and version sign-ins add a few more. Not for Kay: the 60-pull Headhunting Dossier becomes a 10-pull for the next banner.

    How the numbers are worked out

    Progress counts only pulls done, the banner's own counter. Each HH permit is a 500-Oroberyl discount on a pull, so your balance is worth Oroberyl + permits × 500 + Origeometry × 75. Oroberyl still to earn = (120 − pulls done) × 500 − that value.

    Per-day figures divide what's still needed by the time left. The Monthly Pass figure assumes you buy it today, so you get today's login plus each 4:00 reset before the banner ends (30 at most), and the 12 Origeometry.

    Free pace is your average daily gain between entries, with Monthly Pass income removed on days it was active. Pulls you make don't change it, because spent Oroberyl moves into "pulls done". The path to 120 is a straight line from your latest entry to 120 on 23 Dec.

    Changes compare your latest entry with the one before it. Separately, your 6★ pity (80 pulls, 50% chance of Kay) carries over to later banners, but the 120-pull guarantee only applies to this banner.
